# Pastes never go away (#41)

Our pastebox has run for a year and its disk is now 90% full. Nothing is ever removed: pastes are kept until someone deletes them, and almost nobody does.

Could a paste expire? A choice on the form would do: 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, or never.

Please keep "never" as the default, so nothing changes for people who don't pick.
